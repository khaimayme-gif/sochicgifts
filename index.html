Our Story

Born from a belief that gifts create connections

Hello, I'm Miki.

I'm a Myanmar entrepreneur based in Thailand, and gift giving is genuinely my love language. In August 2024, I founded So Chic Gifts because I believe thoughtful gifts can communicate emotions that words alone sometimes cannot.

So Chic is built for people who want their loved ones to feel truly remembered — whether they're across town or across an ocean. We serve Myanmar communities in Thailand and Japan, long-distance couples, students living abroad, and anyone who wants their gift to mean something deeper.
